Vigie theme family — 4 looks

Shared light-mode rule

  • The hardware notch is always black. In both modes the closed island on a notched MacBook keeps the dark ink and dark status set. Light mode only changes the menu bar around it.
  • Panels keep a black band. The peek and expanded panels grow out of the notch, so their top 34 px stays black (wordmark and clock stay on it). The panel body below switches to the light material.
  • Plain displays: the virtual notch style black looks like the hardware notch. The menu_bar style takes the light material and light tokens.
  • Status: five states (idle, working, done, blocked, failed), each with its own shape, label and colour. Light inks reach at least 4.5:1 for text, and glows are dropped on light surfaces.

A · Chrome Hologram · light

  • Material: pearl glass (#F3F5F9) with white cards. The iris filaments and rim move from pastel to deeper cyan, violet and rose so they still show on white.
  • Platinum becomes graphite for ink, the week ring and the PR bar. Shimmer becomes a white specular sweep.
  • Status: pastel tints become deep jewel inks (mint #0E7F57, champagne #985A00, sky #1D6FC0, rose #C8243F, orchid #9B35C4). Same ring, arc, check, diamond and × shapes.

C · Twin Metal Tactical · light

  • Material: brushed aluminium (#E6E9EB) with paler cards. Silver structure (corners, ticks, bezels, hairlines) turns graphite #5A636A.
  • Gold deepens: #A07418 for frames, arcs and markers, and #765709 for gold text (5.5:1). Gold stays reserved for active and now.
  • Status: deep instrument inks (#12703F, #985200, #1462AE, #BE1A1A, #8E2A9E). Glyph shapes are unchanged.
