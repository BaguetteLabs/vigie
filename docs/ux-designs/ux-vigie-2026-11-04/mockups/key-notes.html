Notes and secrets

Notes are markdown. ||inline|| secrets and ```secret blocks render masked. Click a secret to reveal it. A copied secret is cleared from the clipboard after a configurable delay (30 s here). The global notes shortcut opens a floating panel that takes focus. The same notes are listed in the expanded view's Notes tab. Notes sync through iCloud and are encrypted. They never appear in the closed notch.
