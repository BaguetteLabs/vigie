Usage tab

Shown

  • 01 Main: Claude Code (Max) and Codex (Plus), each with 5h + weekly pace bars and a nested ring.
  • 02 Three providers in a scrolling list, in user order: Codex past its 80% threshold, Claude Code on Ultra (no 5h window, so pace runs on weekly), and Gemini CLI (daily quota only).
  • 03 Empty: no usage provider turned on.

Colours (user choices)

  • pace-ahead · user-configurable. Default Ember: dark #FF8A3D (8.2:1) · light #B5410A (5.2:1, text-safe). On pace = theme metal fill.
  • quota-warning · user-configurable. Default Signal yellow: dark #FFE14D · light #8A6D00 (text-safe). Always paired with hatch, triangle and WARN.
  • Time context: Wed 04 Nov 14:48. Elapsed % is computed from each reset time.

Composition choices to confirm

  • Layout (user decision): 50/50. Providers on the left half, History · 7D and Cost · today on the right half.
  • More than 2 providers: the left half becomes a scrolling list in user order. Affordance: bottom fade, a 3px scroll track and the hint DRAG TO REORDER · ALSO IN SETTINGS + SCROLL ↓. Each card header carries a 6-dot grip, reusing the Shelf grip pattern.
  • Ring size: 58px (was 64) to fit the half-width cards. Single-window plans (Ultra weekly, Gemini daily) get one primary-weight ring.
  • Ring: inner = 5h (thicker), outer = weekly (thinner), per DESIGN Quota dial. The Home-card baseline had them the other way round.
  • Ring centre: shows the 5h value, or the only window the plan has.
  • Fill: 5h in bright platinum metal, weekly in darker silver metal. Pace-ahead and warning replace the fill.
  • Pace marker: 2px platinum bar at elapsed time on bars, short radial tick on rings. Threshold: dashed tick at 80% on every bar; value shown in the card meta.
  • Precedence: quota warning wins over pace-ahead on the same window.
  • Quota-warning hue (user decision, own token, user-configurable): default Signal yellow, dark #FFE14D · light #8A6D00 (text-safe). Shared by A and C; replaces the lime first proposed, which looked like C's green "on pace".
  • Ember vs warning: ahead of pace = solid Ember fill, AHEAD OF PACE, no glyph. Quota warning = signal yellow with a hatched fill, a triangle and WARN, plus a triangle on the ring centre.
  • Pace tolerance (user decision): ahead only when used > elapsed + 5 pts (configurable). Inside the band the label reads WITHIN PACE · +3 and the fill stays metal.
  • Strings: WITHIN PACE, AHEAD OF PACE · +9, WITHIN PACE · +3, WARN · ≥ 80%, RESET 16:40 · IN 1H52, ELAPSED 63%, empty NO USAGE PROVIDER.
