Calendar tab

Shown

  • 01 Main: an Itsycal-structured month grid (header with ‹ ● ›, weekday initials, ISO week column, per-calendar dots, today outlined in cyan, a line tracing November's days, adjacent-month days dimmed) and the upcoming list (5 events; the next one is a hero with IN 42 MIN and Join call).
  • 02 A day clicked (Thu 12): the list starts at that day. The header dot lights up as back to today. Also shown: navigating to December.
  • 03 Permission not granted (setup-problem styling) and no events today.

Data

  • Two EventKit calendars: Work and Personal. Dot colours are sample EventKit calendar colours (user data), not Vigie tokens. Time context: Wed 04 Nov 2026 14:48. Weeks start Monday; ISO week numbers.

Composition choices to confirm

  • Grid geometry: 300px month card, 36 × 37px day cells, 22px week column. One dot per calendar with events that day, no event count.
  • Today vs selected: today = cyan 1px rounded outline (A's now colour). Selected day = platinum tint with a hairline. Both show when the selected day is not today.
  • Today dot: dim when today is in view and selected. Cyan and lit when you can jump back (another day selected or another month shown).
  • Month outline: a 1px platinum line at 28% traces the month's days, inside the card.
  • Upcoming list: grouped by day headers. The next event is a hero row (big numeral, cyan countdown, now→event track, JOIN CALL ↵ only when there is a video link). Other rows: time · calendar dot · title (+ video tag) · calendar · relative time. All-day events show as a chip on their day header. 5 events max, then +N LATER.
  • Month navigation (‹ ›) changes only the grid. The list keeps its selected day until a day is clicked.
  • Calendar colours: uses the EventKit colours for dots, as the user asked. They may clash with status hues; a later option could map calendars to metal tones.
  • Permission state: an in-tab setup card with a neutral outline, an i-square mark and two buttons. Must match the setup-problem alert in key-alert-variants.html.
  • No events today: the grid stays, with no dot on today. The list shows NO EVENTS TODAY, then the following days.
