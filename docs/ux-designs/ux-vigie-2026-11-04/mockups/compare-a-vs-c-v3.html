Vigie theme family — Chrome Hologram · Twin Metal Tactical (v3)

Shared base (identical in both columns)

  • Geometry: closed island 540 × 34 (menu-bar height, wings either side of the hardware notch), peek 700 × 150, expanded 720 × 420.
  • Information placement: usage in the left wing, sessions in the right; peek = status, session, pending call, two actions, timer; Home = sessions, usage, shelf, next event, PRs.
  • Components: dial, readout, three shape-coded status glyphs, beacon, action pair, tab bar, cards, meters, tracks. Same slots, same sizes.
  • Motion timing: 280 ms entry · 6 s ambient loop · 1.5 s working · 1.8 s blocked · 3 s peek dwell · 7 s panel scan · 14 s dial bezel · calm = one 2.6 s pulse.
  • Containment: everything is drawn inside the island or panel outline. Peek and expanded may cast a neutral drop shadow, nothing more.

What each variant changes (skin only)

  • A · Chrome Hologram: iridescent glass, pastel status tints, light SF type, rounded pills and cards. Ornament: an iris rim traced inside the island edge plus an interior shimmer. Motion: glides, ripples and shimmer.
  • C · Twin Metal Tactical: gunmetal and gold, spec status hues, condensed caps and mono numerals, square cards with corner marks. Ornament: a tick bezel, inner corner marks and a gold tracer inside the island. Motion: mechanical ticks, tracers and breathing brackets.
Frames are an 820 px crop of the menu bar centred on the notch. The grey desktop and menu bar are plain macOS, not Vigie. Vigie only draws inside the black shape.
